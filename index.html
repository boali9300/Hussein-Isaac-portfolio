01 — Profile

Clear ideas. Precise drawings. Buildable work.

Born in Iraq and shaped by life across the Middle East and the United States, I bring a cross-cultural perspective to architecture. My experiences have strengthened my interest in designing places that support safety, belonging, and everyday life—while respecting the character and needs of the communities they serve.

02 — Design Mission

Architecture for people, place, and possibility.

I believe architecture can improve everyday life by bringing together thoughtful ideas, clear technical thinking, and an understanding of context. My long-term practice vision is to create purposeful, buildable work that responds to people, culture, climate, material, and the character of each place.

Human-centered designSpaces shaped around everyday use, comfort, dignity, and experience.
Context and cultureDesign informed by local character, history, identity, and community.
Technical clarityIdeas translated into coordinated, understandable, and buildable information.
Learning and growthCuriosity, mentorship, and continuous improvement through practice.
