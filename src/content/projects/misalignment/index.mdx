<Chapter n="01" title="Process">
  Made in ARC 659: Spatial Archive with Associate Professor Jason Scroggin. The intentional misalignment of the boxes
  introduced several design elements. The gaps along their sides created opportunities to explore how light enters
  the structure, and they inform circulation and the entry points to the site. The process diagram shows the steps
  and the variety of rotations around the X, Y and Z axes. The plan and section cuts show how light enters the
  building through the shifts and rotations of the boxes. The red highlights mark where the boxes intersect, and
  removing parts of these intersections creates interesting spaces and spatial experiences within the enclosure.
</Chapter>

<Chapter n="02" title="Concept and circulation">
  The archive divides into a public library and a private house, with a courtyard path between them. The library
  gathers a lobby, archive storage, a seminar room, a second-floor reading room and a skylight; the house holds the
  living room, kitchenette, study room and bedroom under its own skylight. The circulation diagram shows how the
  misalignment separates public and private entrances and routes.
</Chapter>

<Figure src={concept} caption="Concept" alt="Exploded diagram of colored boxes: the house (private space) with skylight, bedroom and living room, kitchenette and study room at left; the courtyard path between; and the library (public space) with archive storage, skylight, lobby, second-floor reading room and seminar at right." />
