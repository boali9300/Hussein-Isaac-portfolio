<Chapter n="01" title="Brief">
  In the ARC 750 studio, taught by Gary Rohrbacher, students explore the many ways architectural thinking,
  techniques and formats lead to outcomes across disciplines, scales and media. Working with Leteta Badegama, I was
  assigned the Stahl House, Case Study House #22. We developed construction drawings to understand how the house
  was built, and how its mechanical, plumbing and structural systems were integrated into the design.
</Chapter>

<Chapter n="04" title="Sections and details">
  The building sections and wall sections trace the assembly from roof to pile. The roof is a membrane over rigid
  insulation, a concrete slab and metal deck, carried by W12×26 wide-flange beams on 5×3×5/16 steel angles. The
  W8×36 columns stand on a 6" concrete floor. Along the pool, a cantilevered concrete grade beam carries the floor,
  while the other walls sit on grade beams over 3'×3'×1' footings and 16" diameter piles reaching 24 feet down. The
  details show the beam welded to the column, and the steel plate welded to the column and bolted to the grade
  beam.
</Chapter>
