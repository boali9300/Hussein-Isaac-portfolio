<Chapter n="01" title="Brief">
  The ARC 254 studio, taught by Elizabeth Swanson, asked for an urban monastery in Lexington, Kentucky. Students
  researched monasteries, worked through many design iterations, then evaluated them and chose the strongest to
  develop into a final design.
</Chapter>

<Chapter n="02" title="Plans">
  The monastery fills its block as a series of rooms around planted courts. The lower level holds the shared rooms,
  including a refectory with long tables, smaller rooms with round tables and a large columned hall. On the upper
  level, two rows of small cells line a central corridor, joined by a bridge to rooms of desks and shelving.
</Chapter>

<Chapter n="05" title="Sound study">
  An acoustic study of the 48'-0" × 49'-6" hall. The enlarged plan and Section A-A trace direct and reflected sound
  from the speaker to the seats. A 16'-0" suspended reflector panel near the speaker sends sound toward the back of
  the room. The specifications list each surface's material, sound absorption coefficient and area, and the
  calculations give the room's reverberation time and the minimum width of the reflector panel.
</Chapter>
