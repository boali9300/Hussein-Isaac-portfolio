<Chapter n="04" title="Program">
  The 29,190 sq ft Bloom Therapeutic Complex includes a renovated housing complex and a new building that sits on a
  structural truss and rises nine stories above grade. Raising the building lets the historic lilong courtyard and
  streetscape stay in place. Floors 1–2 hold a therapeutic clinic that combines Eastern and Western medical
  practices. Floors 3–8 hold twelve housing units. Floors 3–4 have two units per floor with the living and sleeping
  modules side by side, for less mobile residents. Floors 5–8 have lofted units with living below and sleeping above,
  so the residents who need the most care live closest to the clinic. On the roof, a teahouse with an intensive
  garden gives a place to relax and enjoy the community, away from the pace and pressures of city life. The
  teahouse's north and south walls are glass, carried up from the inside face of the double-skin façade. The
  garden's glass panels run on a pulley system and lift like a garage door, opening the garden to the summer
  weather and closing against winter winds and temperature extremes. Like the rest of the complex, the teahouse
  uses passive heating.
</Chapter>

<Chapter n="05" title="Façade performance">
  The diamonds in the façade are glass panels, each sized to give its floor the best views. Honeycomb solar panels,
  set at 68 degrees, keep out too much radiation and heat in summer. In winter the building invites the sun in: the
  panels seal shut to catch its rays and trap heat in the double-skin, ventilated façade. The trapped heat helps
  condition the building, while excess heat vents at the top of the façade and pre-heats the teahouse. A
  retractable glazing condition opens sweeping views of downtown Shanghai and the Bund while keeping the interior
  comfortable.
</Chapter>

<Chapter n="06" title="Housing units">
  On floors 3–4, each Unit A places its living module and sleeping module side by side on one level. A point of
  refuge at each end of the floor sits next to an exit stair. On floors 5–8, Unit B is a two-story loft with the
  living module below and bedrooms with balconies above.
</Chapter>

  two yoga rooms, a pharmacy, washrooms and a healing garden. The second floor holds diagnostic rooms, treatment
  rooms for acupuncture and cupping, an exercise room, an office and a second healing garden.
